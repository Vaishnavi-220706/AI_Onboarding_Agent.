# Support Escalation Guide

## 1
For normal technical issues, create a ticket in the Internal Support Portal. Include a short description, affected system, time of occurrence, and relevant error message.

## 2
Security incidents, lost devices, suspected account compromise, and phishing reports should be marked as security-related and escalated to Security Operations immediately.

## 3
If a support ticket remains blocked after the initial support response, the employee may ask their manager to escalate the ticket. Critical business-impacting incidents should be escalated as soon as they are identified.

## 4
The Internal Support Portal is the approved channel for technical support and onboarding blockers.
