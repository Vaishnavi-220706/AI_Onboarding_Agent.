# Product FAQ

## 1
The Analytics Dashboard provides company reporting views for approved users. Access to the dashboard is controlled by user permissions.

## 2
The Company Workspace is the standard collaboration environment for employees. It is used for approved internal documents, team communication, and shared work.

## 3
The Support Portal is used to submit and track technical support tickets. It is not a storage location for confidential business documents.

## 4
Product orientation introduces the Analytics Dashboard, Company Workspace, and Support Portal. Feature-specific access may require separate approval.
