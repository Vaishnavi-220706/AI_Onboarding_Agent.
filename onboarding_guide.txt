# Employee Onboarding Guide

## 1
New employees should complete the onboarding checklist during their first week. The checklist includes identity verification, setting up multi-factor authentication, reviewing the security policy, and completing the product orientation.

## 2
The standard support channel for onboarding questions is the Internal Support Portal. Employees should create a support ticket when they need technical assistance or when an onboarding step is blocked.

## 3
New employees receive a company email account and access to the standard employee workspace after their onboarding identity verification is complete.

## 4
Managers are responsible for confirming that a new employee has completed required onboarding steps. Managers should escalate unresolved onboarding blockers through the Internal Support Portal.
